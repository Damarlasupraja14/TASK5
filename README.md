# Tips Dataset - Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

## Dataset Overview
This project involves performing exploratory data analysis (EDA) on the popular 'Tips' dataset, which contains information about restaurant bills, tips, customer gender, smoking behavior, day, time, and party size.

## Objectives
- Analyze customer tipping behavior.
- Understand the influence of gender, smoking status, and day of the week on tips.
- Identify correlations between total bill, tip, and group size.

## Key Visualizations
- Countplots for categorical variables like gender, smoker status, and day.
- Barplots comparing total bill and tip amounts by gender and day.
- Scatterplots showing relationships between total bill, tip, and size.
- Boxplots to explore the distribution of numerical variables.
- Histograms to understand the distribution of tips and total bills.
- Heatmap to observe correlation between numerical features.

## Key Findings
- Most customers were male and non-smokers.
- Saturday saw the highest number of customers and tips.
- Tips increase with the total bill and group size.
- Most tips range from $2 to $4; total bills are usually between $10 and $20.
- Males tend to tip slightly more than females on average.

## Tools Used
- Python
- Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook
